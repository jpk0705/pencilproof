## YouTube explainer — 2–4 minutes

Title: How to Compare Two Car Dealer Quotes Without Getting Tricked by the Payment

Opening:

“Two payments can look different because the deals are different, or simply because the financing assumptions are different. Normalize the inputs before deciding which quote is better.”

Outline and script:

1. “Confirm both quotes identify the same vehicle, trim, equipment, and mileage.”
2. “Compare selling price before cash down or trade equity changes the presentation.”
3. “Separate government charges, dealer fees, and optional products.”
4. “Use the same trade allowance and payoff, or temporarily remove the trade from both comparisons.”
5. “Match cash down, APR, and term. A longer term can lower the payment while increasing total cost.”
6. “Compare amount financed and total of payments, then decide which differences you actually value.”

Worked example:

“Quote A finances $29,400 for 60 months. Quote B finances $31,740 for 72 months. Quote B may display the lower payment, but it begins with $2,340 more debt and keeps the loan open longer. Ask both dealers for the same APR, term, cash, and trade assumptions.”
