Cash down and total due at signing are not always the same number. In this fictional example, two thousand dollars is the down payment. The first payment adds six hundred twenty dollars, and registration adds four hundred ten. That makes three thousand thirty due at signing. Ask the dealer to itemize the difference in writing, then confirm those charges were not counted again in the amount financed. The payment alone cannot answer that.
