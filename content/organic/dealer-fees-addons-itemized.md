## YouTube explainer — 2–4 minutes

Title: Dealer Fees and Add-Ons: The Three Questions to Ask Before Signing

Opening:

“If a dealer describes a product only as a few dollars per month, convert the conversation back to total price. You are deciding whether to buy the product—not merely whether the payment sounds manageable.”

Outline and script:

1. “Separate government charges, dealer fees, and optional products into three groups.”
2. “Ask whether each line is required by law, required by a lender, charged by the dealer, or optional.”
3. “Request the full cash price, term, provider, deductible, exclusions, and cancellation terms for each product.”
4. “If you decline something, ask for a revised amount financed and payment while APR, term, trade, and cash remain unchanged.”
5. “Compare the revised buyer’s order with the final contract. A verbal promise does not replace the written figures.”

Worked example:

“An $85 documentation fee, $2,295 service contract, and $700 GAP product total $3,080 before finance charges. The payment difference depends on APR and term, but the purchase decision starts with the $3,080 price.”
