## Buyer lesson

A worksheet can show $2,000 cash down while asking for $3,030 at signing. That is not automatically an error: the total may also include the first payment, registration, or other itemized charges. The problem is when the difference is not written clearly. Ask for one line showing cash down and a separate itemized total due at signing. Never assume the two numbers mean the same thing.

Fictional example: $2,000 cash down + $620 first payment + $410 registration = $3,030 total due. Confirm whether every listed amount is also reflected correctly in the amount financed, so nothing is counted twice.
