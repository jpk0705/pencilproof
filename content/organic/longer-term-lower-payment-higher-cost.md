## YouTube explainer — 2–4 minutes

Title: Longer Car Loan Terms: Lower Payment, Higher Total Cost?

Explain that term is the number of scheduled payments, and that a lower monthly payment does not by itself mean a cheaper deal. Walk through the fixed $30,000 / 7.50% fictional example, then show why the comparison must also hold vehicle, price, fees, products, trade, cash down, and APR constant. Close by asking viewers to compare the written amount financed and total of payments.
